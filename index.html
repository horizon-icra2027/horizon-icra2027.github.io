HORIZON: Holistic Operating Room Intelligence with Zero-Barrier Orchestration and Networking

Full-day workshop · IEEE ICRA 2027, Seoul, Korea · 24 or 28 May 2027 (date to be assigned)

Researchers, surgeons, hospital leaders, and companies come together to integrate their views, so that they can collaborate coherently in the future operating room.

About

Surgical robots and AI are reaching patients, yet each component still enters the operating room (OR) as an island. Standards and smart-OR platforms exist, but stakeholders rarely agree on what each will provide and accept before integration choices lock in.

HORIZON treats the OR as one robotic system whose robots, sensors, devices, and people must collaborate coherently, and aims to integrate stakeholders' views towards that collaboration. Where stakeholders seek consensus on details, a shared outline of the procedure's workflow and data flow serves as a common reference. This is core ICRA territory — task allocation, human–robot interaction, shared autonomy, and multi-agent coordination — and transfers to other surgical domains.

All talks share one application, posterior-segment ophthalmic interventions, and one topic, component integration into the ophthalmic OR.

Orchestration
Negotiating the OR's structure among all stakeholders.
Zero-barrier
Removing barriers to safe interconnection, never clinical safeguards or surgical authority.
Protocol
A provisional consensus on how stakeholders' capabilities interconnect and remain compatible.

How the day works

  1. Talks. Each talk covers achievements, what the speaker's group will provide, and what it expects from other stakeholders. Clinicians give critical reviews of robotic surgery cases. Every talk ends with one offer/need slide.
  2. Offer–need matrix. Offers and needs are collected live, by workflow phase, item, time horizon, and maturity.
  3. Roundtables. The technical roundtable forms a first shared view; the final roundtable matches offers and needs, with live polls, and discusses how surgeons preserve their authority as robotic automation grows.
  4. HORIZON protocol draft v0.1. A living document, revised with participants at 3, 6, and 12 months.
